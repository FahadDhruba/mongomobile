# Code of conduct

This is a short code of conduct for MongoMobile. It applies in issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other space for the project.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept criticism of work, not of people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual
  attention or comments.
- Do not publish other people's private information without permission.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests,
or block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through a private message on
GitHub or a private security advisory on this repository. Reports are
treated confidentially.
